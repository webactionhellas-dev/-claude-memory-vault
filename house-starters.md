**Motion stack (added 2026-07-10):** the marketing starter is the home of the GSAP+Lenis catalog primitives in `components/motion/`: SmoothScroll, Reveal, SplitReveal, MagneticButton + new HorizontalScroll (pin+snap), Marquee (scroll-velocity kinetic), Pin (chaptered storytelling), CustomCursor (quickTo). All reduced-motion aware + headless-safe; `tsc --noEmit` clean. The **commerce starter is deliberately kept lean** (near-zero-JS: a vanilla IO reveal that is intentionally disabled, a global reduced-motion block, a CSS marquee) because a storefront's LCP/bundle beats spectacle and its cart drawer/overlays would hit the transform-ancestor position:fixed trap under a wrapped smooth-scroller. Do NOT bloat commerce with GSAP; port the marketing primitives only when a specific build needs the premium tier. This decision is recorded in the house-playbook starters section.
