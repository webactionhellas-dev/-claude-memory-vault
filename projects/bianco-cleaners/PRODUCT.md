# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Residents of Athens who need professional dry and wet cleaning services. Primary use case: discovering services, checking hours/pricing, and calling to book a pickup or visit the store. Secondary audience: students (discount offer) and customers with special garments (wedding dresses, leathers, luxury items). Users are typically on mobile mid-errand or on desktop while researching options.

## Product Purpose

Bianco Cleaners is a professional dry and wet cleaning service in Athens, Greece, with over 30 years of experience. They use Jet Clean technology and eco-friendly antibacterial products, certified ISO 9001. The site exists to build trust, communicate service offerings clearly, and drive phone call conversions. Success = a phone call to 210 77 97 290.

## Brand Personality

Warm, meticulous, trustworthy. Greek family-business pride combined with ISO-certified professional quality. Approachable and personal, not cold or corporate.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-generated SaaS landing pages. Cold corporate minimalism. Anything that looks like a software product rather than a trusted local service.

## Design Principles

1. Trust first — certifications, experience, and social proof must be visible immediately and specifically
2. Warmth without casualness — professional but personal; never sterile or transactional
3. The phone number is the product — every section leads back to one clear conversion action
4. Specificity over claims — concrete services, real prices, actual hours beat "quality" adjectives
5. Greek pride — this is an Athens institution; local identity and longevity are brand strengths

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Greek-language content throughout. Keyboard navigable, screen reader friendly.
