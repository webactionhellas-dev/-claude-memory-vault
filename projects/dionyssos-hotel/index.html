The Dionyssos

For three generations of summers, guests have climbed the same stone steps from the harbour — to a garden of pines and bougainvillea, a pool that holds the sky, and rooms that open onto the rooftops of one of Greece's most beautiful towns.

No lobbies of marble, no formality for its own sake. Just the island, arranged comfortably around you.

The Setting

A terrace of gardens between the town and the sea

The Dionyssos stands just above Skopelos Town, built in the island's own manner — whitewashed walls, timber balconies, terracotta roofs — around a quiet pool terrace shaded by palms and old pines.

Weddings & Celebrations

Married by the water, under the island sky

Candlelight on still water, tables under the palms, and the town glittering below. The Dionyssos has hosted Skopelos weddings for decades — and still treats each one as the only one.

The Island

Skopelos, the green island of the Aegean

Pine forests that run all the way to the water, a town of white houses and slate roofs stacked above the harbour, and beaches the cinema made famous but the ferries kept honest. You are five minutes from all of it.

Ask us anything — we grew up here

  • The Old Town & Harbour

    Alleys of whitewash and bougainvillea, tavernas on the waterfront, and over a hundred churches between them.

    5 min walk

  • Agios Ioannis Chapel

    The chapel on the rock from Mamma Mia! — 198 steps, and a view that explains the whole film.

    30 min drive

  • Stafilos & Panormos

    Pine-fringed bays of clear, calm water — the island's beloved swimming beaches, minutes from town.

    10–20 min drive

  • The Pine Forests

    Skopelos is the greenest island in the Aegean — walking trails leave from town and disappear into resin-scented shade.
