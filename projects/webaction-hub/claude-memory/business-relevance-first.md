Every visual, 3D element, animation, image and section must be ABOUT THE BUSINESS: what they sell, what they do, their real content, brand and offering. Never abstract or off-topic decoration.

**Why:** Mike repeatedly rejected work as "useless" and "generic": stock photos instead of the client's own, and abstract floating orbs/particles that "have nothing to do with dogs." He is deliberately training the fleet to be fully customized to the business every single time. Off-topic decoration reads as generic AI slop and destroys the value, no matter how technically polished.

**How to apply:** Source the business's REAL logo, photos, reviews, location, socials and services FIRST, and build everything from those. For a dog groomer, 3D and motion depict dogs, grooming, paws, their real space and services, NOT abstract shapes (no orbs, particles, blobs, balls, gradient meshes). Before adding any element, ask "what does this have to do with what they sell?" If the answer is nothing, cut it. This is now a house invariant (house-playbook) and a design-standards hard rule. See [[nami-design-standards]] and [[underdog-site]].
