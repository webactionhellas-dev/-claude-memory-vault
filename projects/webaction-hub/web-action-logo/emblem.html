What changed

Type with intent

The monogram is now set in Cormorant Garamond — a high-contrast serif with genuine character — and the ampersand is an italic, slightly-raised accent so “W&A” reads as a crafted ligature, not three flat letters. Caps and tagline use Jost, a clean geometric sans, widely tracked.

Structure & balance

A two-weight hairline ring replaces the single heavy circle for depth, the monogram is optically centred, and “WEB ACTION” keeps its flanking rules but with corrected tracking and length. The badge stays perfectly legible from a 32px favicon up to signage.
