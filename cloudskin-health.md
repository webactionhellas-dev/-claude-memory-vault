## Health check 2026-08-03

Live and healthy. `cloudskin.com` still 308-redirects to canonical `www.cloudskin.com`, 200 OK, ~1.0s. Gate page weighs 493 KB over 2 requests, ~1.8-2.4s to network-idle. No broken images, no horizontal overflow, desktop or mobile.

**Still fully gated and noindexed, confirm this is intended.** Every route returns 200 and serves distinct content (`/` gate, `/home` storefront, `/collection`, `/product`, `/about`), but all of them carry `robots: noindex, nofollow`, and `/` is titled "CLOUDSKIN(R) . Launching Soon" with a password field. Loading `/home` in a real browser aborts all 22 of its own requests and lands back on the gate, so the storefront is unreachable without the password. Per [[cloudskin-stripe-golive]] the custom Stripe checkout is LIVE, so if the intent is to be selling, the site is currently invisible to search and blocked to the public. This is a business-decision item for Mike, not a defect.

**Gate is client-side only.** `curl https://www.cloudskin.com/home` returns the full storefront HTML (title "CLOUDSKIN(R) . Luxury Activewear | Tennis . Padel . Studio"), as does `/collection`. Only the JS redirect enforces the gate. Pre-launch pricing and layout are therefore readable by anyone who fetches without JS. Low urgency while pre-launch, but do not treat the gate as access control.

**Actionable finding, WCAG AA contrast fail.** `#notify > button[type="submit"]` (the NOTIFY ME submit on the gate) renders `#8f6e41` on `#e7e3d9`, ratio 3.66:1 at 11px/normal. Needs 4.5:1. This is the single conversion control on the only public page, so it is worth fixing. A further 10 elements sit over image/gradient and could not be auto-verified, check by eye.

**Actionable finding, console error from CSP.** One console error on desktop: the `fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces...&family=Inter...` request is blocked by `connect-src 'self' https://*.supabase.co ...`. Verified this is cosmetic, not a font failure: `style-src` does allow `https://fonts.googleapis.com`, the stylesheet loads, and `design_audit.mjs` confirms "Inter is loaded and rendering on Latin headings". The blocked request is the speculative/preconnect fetch. Fix by adding `https://fonts.googleapis.com` to `connect-src` to clear the DevTools noise.

**Live security headers, all present and correct**: CSP (full directive set incl. `object-src 'none'`, `base-uri 'self'`, `frame-ancestors 'self'`), HSTS `max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`, `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, `Permissions-Policy` locking camera/mic/geolocation/interest-cohort.
